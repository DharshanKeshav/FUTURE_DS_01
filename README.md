***# FUTURE_DS_01***

***“Designed and implemented an interactive Power BI sales dashboard for the Future Interns Data Science Internship (Task 1), focusing on revenue trends, product performance, and regional sales insights.”****

***🛠️ Tools Used:***

Power BI Desktop: Used for end-to-end BI workflow: Data Cleaning, Modeling, and Visualization.

Power Query: To clean and transform raw sales data (handling nulls, formatting dates).

DAX (Data Analysis Expressions): Created measures for Total Revenue, Year-over-Year (YoY) Growth, and Profit Margins.

****🗝️ Key Insights****

Revenue Trends: Identified the highest revenue-generating months and seasonal peaks in sales.

Product Analysis: Highlighted the top 5 products contributing to over 40% of the total profit.

Geographic Distribution: Visualized sales performance across different regions, identifying underperforming markets that require strategic focus.

****🚀 How to View the Project****

Download the Future intern_DS_1.pbix file from the folder.

Open it using Power BI Desktop.
